Energy fixing questions - quick answers

Should I fix my energy tariff now?

There is no single answer for every household. The useful comparison is your own fixed or Tracker rates against the price-cap path, using your region and expected consumption. This tool does that month by month.

Are energy prices expected to rise in January 2027?

For the week beginning 12 October 2026, the MoneySavingExpert average forecast for January-March 2027 is a 25% rise from the October cap. It is still an early forecast rather than a confirmed Ofgem cap.

Does the Energy Price Cap limit my total bill?

No. It limits unit rates and standing charges on standard variable tariffs. The amount you actually pay still depends on your energy use.

Why does winter usage matter?

Annual usage is not divided evenly by 12. Gas use is heavily weighted into winter using DESNZ seasonal data, while electricity uses a gentler monthly profile. That means a price rise arriving in winter can affect a gas-heated household more than an annual-average figure suggests.

What does the calculator compare?

It compares the selected UW fixed tariff, matching UW Tracker tariff and regional price cap. Forecast outputs are personalised estimates rather than an official UW quote or a guarantee of future prices.
